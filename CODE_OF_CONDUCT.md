# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in 1904 Desktop. This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces maintained for the project.

Treat people with respect. Discuss the work, give specific feedback, and allow room for different levels of experience and different viewpoints. Respect a request to stop an interaction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, and sharing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Report a concern

Report conduct concerns privately through [1904's contact form](https://1904.ai/contact). Include relevant links and enough detail for maintainers to understand what happened. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation based on the severity or recurrence of the behavior. Retaliation for reporting a concern is unacceptable.
